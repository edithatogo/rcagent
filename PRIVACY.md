# Privacy Notice for Distributed Skill Packages

The repository-authored `rca-investigation` skill and its skills-only client
packages contain no analytics, telemetry, network client, account integration,
credential store, persistent data store, model endpoint, or remote service.
They do not themselves collect or transmit personal information.

The skill may be invoked inside a client platform. That platform's data use,
logging, retention, telemetry, model processing, administrator controls, and
privacy terms apply independently. Installing this package does not approve
the use of private clinical, patient, consumer, employee, credential,
confidential, or organisation-sensitive data with that platform.

Use generated-synthetic placeholders by default. Any use of real or
potentially identifiable material requires a separately authorised governed
pathway. De-identification reduces risk but does not guarantee anonymity.

Public issue reports and support requests must not contain private or
confidential information. See `SUPPORT.md` for the reporting boundary.
