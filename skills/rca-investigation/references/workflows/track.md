# Action and effectiveness workflow

1. Map each accepted recommendation to its finding and intended safety
   mechanism.
2. Define a specific action, accountable owner, authority, resources,
   dependencies, due date, implementation evidence, and escalation path.
3. Prefer elimination, substitution, forcing functions, redesign, automation,
   simplification, and resilient barriers where proportionate. Explain any
   reliance on training, reminders, or policy alone.
4. Define outcome, process, balancing, and equity measures with baseline,
   target, data source, review interval, and interpretation limits.
5. Separate status:
   `proposed`, `accepted`, `in progress`, `implemented`, `effectiveness
   unverified`, `effective`, `ineffective`, `superseded`, or `closed with
   residual risk`.
6. Review unintended consequences, workarounds, control degradation, and
   whether risk moved elsewhere.
7. Escalate overdue, ineffective, unevidenced, or ownerless actions through
   the approved governance system.

Use `assets/templates/markdown/11-capa-action-plan.md` for the working record
and `assets/templates/markdown/13-investigation-closure.md` for a closure
proposal. Never infer closure from action completion alone.
