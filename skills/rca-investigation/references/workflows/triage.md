# Triage workflow

Use this workflow to establish a safe investigation scope, not to make an
authoritative clinical, legal, regulatory, or employment determination.

1. Record the event description, known outcome, detection route, current risk,
   and immediate safeguards separately.
2. Confirm the applicable jurisdiction, organisation, policy revision, and
   authorised decision-maker. Do not silently translate severity schemes
   between organisations.
3. Identify notification and open-disclosure questions for authorised human
   review. Mark deadlines as unverified until checked against current policy.
4. Establish the investigation purpose, scope, exclusions, team, conflicts of
   interest, consumer or family participation, staff support, records handling,
   and decision authority.
5. List available and missing evidence. Preserve volatile evidence through the
   approved system rather than copying it into an uncontrolled workspace.
6. Select proportionate methods using
   `references/method-selection-matrix.md`.
7. Produce terms of reference with `assets/templates/markdown/01-investigation-tor.md`.

Handoff only after immediate safety risks and evidence-preservation needs have
an owner. Label all provisional classifications and unverified obligations.
