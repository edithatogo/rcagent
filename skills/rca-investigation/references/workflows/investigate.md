# Investigation workflow

1. Confirm authority, scope, evidence boundary, participants, support, and
   conflicts before interviewing or analysing.
2. Build a source-linked chronology with
   `assets/templates/markdown/02-chronology.md`. Distinguish documentary
   evidence, accounts, inferences, gaps, and discrepancies.
3. Describe expected work and work as done at the time, including workload,
   environment, equipment, interfaces, coordination, resources, policy
   usability, adaptations, and governance.
4. Select the smallest complementary method set. Read only the required files
   under `references/methods/`.
5. For each candidate contributing condition, record supporting evidence,
   conflicting evidence, alternative explanations, uncertainty, and system
   level.
6. Analyse controls and barriers: intended function, presence, usability,
   operation, monitoring, degradation, and interactions.
7. Test whether findings explain the event without relying on hindsight,
   outcome bias, unsupported counterfactuals, or individual blame.
8. Draft recommendations tied to evidenced findings and specify anticipated
   mechanism, implementation evidence, effectiveness measures, balancing
   measures, and residual-risk review.

Stop and return an evidence-gap plan when material conclusions cannot be
supported. Explicitly require authorised human review before accepting a
finding. Keep participant accounts in their own voice, separate each account
from analysis and findings, and do not resolve conflicts by averaging,
blending, or silently selecting one account. State the unresolved uncertainty.
