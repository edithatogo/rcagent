# PPTX Generation: Executive Summary Deck

Use the document-skills:pptx skill to generate this presentation.
Apply styles from: assets/styles/pptx-style-guide.md

## Purpose
Board/executive briefing. Maximum 6 slides. Plain language. Strategic focus — what happened, why it matters, what we are doing.

## Slide Structure (6 slides)

### Slide 1: Title
- Title: "Serious Adverse Event — Executive Briefing"
- SAC badge (prominent, coloured)
- Date | Classification | Incident ID

### Slide 2: Key Message
- Single large text block (Calibri Bold 36pt)
- 2–3 sentences: what happened and why it matters at an organisational level
- Example: "A patient experienced [harm] due to gaps in [system]. This reveals a systemic risk across [scope]. We are taking [high-level action] to address this."

### Slide 3: Risk Level
- 5×5 risk matrix with current position marked
- Before (inherent risk) and After (expected residual risk after actions)
- One-line rationale for risk level

### Slide 4: Critical Findings
- Card layout (2–3 cards)
- Each card: one finding, one implication, one action
- Focus on organisational-level findings — not clinical detail

### Slide 5: Actions
- Table: Action | When | Who | Expected Outcome
- 3–5 actions maximum
- Emphasise strong actions over weak ones

### Slide 6: Monitoring and Assurance
- How the board/executive will know actions have worked
- Review schedule
- Escalation trigger: "We will re-brief if..."
- Endorsement request
