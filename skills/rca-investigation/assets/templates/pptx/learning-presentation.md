# PPTX Generation: Learning Presentation

Use the document-skills:pptx skill to generate this presentation.
Apply styles from: assets/styles/pptx-style-guide.md

## Purpose
De-identified staff learning presentation. Maximum de-identification. No patient identifiers. No staff names. Shared at team level for learning — not for governance records.

## Slide Structure (8–10 slides)

### Slide 1: Title
- Title: "Learning from [de-identified event description]"
- Subtitle: "[Service name — de-identified if appropriate] | [Month/Year]"
- Style: Safety-II positive framing — "What can we learn to keep our patients safer?"

### Slide 2: Our Patient
- De-identified case summary: clinical context only, no identifiers
- Why this matters: patient impact (factual, respectful)

### Slide 3: What Happened
- Simplified timeline — key moments only (not full investigation chronology)
- Visual: horizontal arrow with 4–5 key milestones

### Slide 4: What We Found — The System Picture
- Insert simplified fishbone or Yorkshire factors diagram
- Focus on system factors — not individual actions
- Language: "The system allowed this to happen" not "Person X failed"

### Slide 5: What Usually Goes Right
- Safety-II lens: "How does this process usually work well?"
- What was different this time?
- Acknowledge the skill and effort of staff in difficult conditions

### Slide 6: What We Are Changing
- 3–5 specific changes with plain-language descriptions
- Include: what changes, who it affects, when it happens
- Distinguish: already done vs. in progress vs. planned

### Slide 7: How This Affects Your Practice
- Specific behavioural changes for this audience
- New process/checklist/policy — show the change concretely
- Q&A prompts

### Slide 8: Discussion
- 3 reflective questions for team discussion
- "What do you do in similar situations that keeps patients safe?"
- "Are there other risks we should know about?"
- How to report: link to incident reporting system
