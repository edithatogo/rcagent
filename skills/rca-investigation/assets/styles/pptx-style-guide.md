# PPTX Style Guide — Healthcare RCA Presentations

## Colour Palette
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Primary / Dark: Dark Blue (#1F3864)
- Secondary: Medium Blue (#2E75B6)
- Accent 1: Teal (#008080)
- Accent 2: Light Blue (#9DC3E6)
- Warning: Orange (#ED7D31)
- Critical: Dark Red (#C00000)
- Success: Green (#70AD47)
- Text: Near-Black (#262626)

## Slide Master
- Font: Calibri throughout
- Title font: Calibri Bold 32pt, Dark Blue
- Body font: Calibri 20pt, Near-Black
- Maximum 5 bullet points per content slide
- Minimum font size: 18pt (for accessibility)

## Slide Types

### Title Slide
- Full-width Dark Blue bar occupying top 40% of slide
- Organisation logo: white version, top right within blue bar
- Title: Calibri Bold 40pt, White, centred in blue bar
- Subtitle: Calibri 24pt, White, below title
- Date and classification: bottom left, Calibri 14pt, dark grey
- "CONFIDENTIAL: QUALITY IMPROVEMENT" badge: red rounded rectangle, bottom right

### Section Divider Slide
- Full Dark Blue background
- Section number: Calibri Light 60pt, white, left
- Section title: Calibri Bold 36pt, white, right-aligned

### Content Slide (standard)
- White background
- Dark Blue left border bar (1cm width)
- Title: Calibri Bold 28pt, Dark Blue, top
- Content area: Calibri 20pt, left-aligned bullets
- Slide number and "CONFIDENTIAL" footer

### Key Finding / Card Slide
- 2–3 cards across the slide
- Each card: Light Blue (#DEEAF1) background, rounded corners
- Card heading: Calibri Bold 18pt, Dark Blue
- Card body: Calibri 16pt, Near-Black

### Timeline Slide
- Horizontal arrow across slide, Dark Blue
- Milestone markers: coloured circles (orange = warning sign, red = event, green = resolution)
- Date labels below, Calibri 14pt
- Description above, Calibri 14pt

### Risk Matrix Slide
- 5×5 grid, colour-coded (green to red)
- Current risk position marked with large dot
- Before/after comparison if showing improvement

### Table Slide
- Full-width table with Dark Blue header row
- Maximum 5 data rows (use multiple slides for larger tables)
- Status colour coding applied in cells

## Footer (all slides)
- Left: Organisation name, Calibri 12pt, grey
- Centre: Date, Calibri 12pt, grey
- Right: "CONFIDENTIAL: QUALITY IMPROVEMENT" | Slide number

## Accessibility
- All colour coding must also use shape or text label (not colour alone)
- Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1
- Alt text on all images and diagrams
