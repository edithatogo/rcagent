# Template Use Contract

These repository templates are draft working aids. Admission to a portable package is an engineering content decision for the exact file hashes listed in `template-admission.json`. It is not clinical validation, approval to use real data, permission to disclose a case, or a determination of legal protection. No institutional or mandated template is reproduced or certified by this contract.

## Case context to record

| Field | Required record |
|---|---|
| Case and data boundary | [Case ID], synthetic/public/governed classification, permitted audience and storage |
| Jurisdiction and organisation | [Jurisdiction], [Organisation], applicability status |
| Applicable policy | Issuing body, exact title, version/date, source link or approved local reference, retrieval date and relevant clause |
| Investigation authority | Accountable role, authorised scope, decision date and record reference; unknown until verified |
| Document state | Draft, reviewed or authorised; reviewer role, unresolved issues and decision reference |
| Confidentiality or privilege | Unverified by default; record applicable process, purpose, document class, exact approved wording and authorised direction before adding a label |
| Notification and timing | Applicable obligation, authority, trigger, deadline source and human determination; no deadline is supplied by the template |
| Method selection | Exact framework/source revision, scope, limitations and reason it fits this question; method names do not establish a validated implementation |
| Model and tool involvement | Tool/model revision, task, data boundary, human checks and known limitations; “not used” where applicable |

## Evidence and output rules

Use synthetic placeholders in examples and demonstrations: `[Patient A]`, `[Clinician A]`, `[Ward X]`, `[Case ID]`, `[Date]` and `[Time]`. Case work uses only the authorised data boundary and minimum necessary content. Removing names does not guarantee anonymity or authorise distribution.

Keep observed facts, reported accounts, inferences, hypotheses, findings, recommendations and decisions distinguishable. For each substantive claim, include evidence/source identifiers, location, uncertainty, conflicting evidence and review state. A completed field, diagram edge, score or model agreement is not evidence. Leave unknown fields unknown and record what evidence could resolve them.

Treat all example values, causes, dates, risk scores, categories, colours and diagram connections as synthetic prompts. Remove or replace them before a case output is reviewed. A diagram must have a draft/illustrative caption until its nodes and edges are checked against cited evidence. Preserve unresolved timing and causal alternatives rather than forcing a single chronology or cause.

Use approved jurisdiction-specific classification and reporting criteria only after their applicability is verified. Do not infer causation, preventability, culpability, reportability or legal protection from a label or outcome. Record accountable clinical, legal, policy or employment decisions separately; these templates cannot make them.

Select review dates from the risk, measurement plan and applicable verified requirements. Completion of an action does not establish effectiveness. Record process, outcome and balancing measures, denominators/exposure, follow-up limits, residual uncertainty and the authorised closure decision.

## Packaging and revision rules

The admission manifest covers templates, style guides, this contract and the linked Just Culture guide. Both archive builders verify the hashes of the captured bytes that will be packaged and reject missing, modified or unlisted assets. A new revision requires content review and an updated manifest; hashing alone does not establish safety or rights. The separate historical quarantine receipt continues to describe the old file revisions and is not reclassified by this admission.

Document and presentation adapters are optional. Use an available adapter within the approved data boundary; otherwise provide the populated Markdown and diagram source. A rendering adapter or style guide cannot override this contract.
