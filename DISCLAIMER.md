# Disclaimer and Approval Boundaries

This project provides research, quality-improvement, investigation-support,
and documentation tools. It does not provide clinical care or replace an
appropriately qualified professional, accountable decision-maker, authorised
review process, or system of record.

## Clinical

Outputs are not diagnoses, treatment recommendations, medical-device outputs,
or evidence of clinical validation. Clinical interpretation and action require
appropriately qualified human review under the applicable governed pathway.

## Policy and regulation

Summaries, mappings, prompts, and templates do not establish that a policy,
standard, regulation, deadline, notification rule, or mandated workflow applies
to a particular organisation, jurisdiction, event, or person. Verify current
authoritative sources and local applicability before use.

## Legal and privilege

Nothing in this package is legal advice or creates confidentiality, statutory
protection, legal professional privilege, or immunity. Labels and workflow
state do not establish those protections. Obtain authorised legal or governance
review where required.

## Organisational approval

Installation, validation, release, or marketplace availability does not mean
that any health service, employer, regulator, professional body, standards
organisation, client vendor, or other organisation has approved, endorsed, or
adopted this project. Local deployment, branding, workflow changes, disclosure,
and operational use require the organisation's own accountable approval.

## Human authority and limitations

Automated output may be incomplete, incorrect, stale, biased, or inapplicable.
It must expose uncertainty, evidence, limitations, and required review. It must
not make clinical, legal, employment, disciplinary, disclosure, regulatory, or
organisational decisions.

## Privacy and data

Public distributions must contain no private clinical, patient, consumer,
employee, workforce, credential, secret, or organisation-confidential data.
Do not submit such data to a client, model, service, marketplace, or registry
without a separately approved governed pathway. De-identification reduces risk
but does not guarantee anonymity.

## Rights and third-party material

Public distributions must not bundle third-party-controlled material unless a
release-specific rights and attribution review records permission or a licence
that permits that distribution. References to external methods, standards,
policies, products, or organisations do not imply ownership, endorsement, or a
right to redistribute their content.

## Release status

A local archive, checksum, passing test, tag, listing, or submission does not by
itself establish publication, acceptance, compatibility, safety, effectiveness,
legal compliance, or organisational approval. Verify the exact release and its
current evidence.
