## Smallest adequate intervention

Use existing Conductor, GitHub, uv, Python, JSON Schema conventions, and the
repository validator. Add only safety-specific queue, context, receipt,
dependency, recovery, and decision semantics that those systems do not supply.
Keep hosted integration optional and fail closed when its evidence is
unavailable. Do not create a replacement incident, identity, content, workflow,
clinical, or records system.

failure isolation, expiry/removal condition, and replacement path.
