limits through caller overrides, miss portable path aliases and accept invalid
ownership timestamps. Context and receipt checks accepted malformed fields and
expired context. These now fail closed with positive and negative fixtures.

review, rework or resume state. The new checkpoint coordinator persists those
transitions, binds outcomes to receipt/artefact hashes and exposes the next
instruction through the workbench CLI. Review found and fixed a nested
wait-during-rework cursor error, unsafe receipt paths and missing positive
stale-lock recovery.

## Agent panel and limitations

Three agents reviewed acceptance/recovery, evidence integrity and safety. Their
instructions required concrete defects, preserved negative evidence and no
completion from checkboxes. Exact model revisions were unavailable; shared
context and correlated errors limit independence. Findings were incorporated
and re-reviewed. All three cleared the implemented recovery within its stated
scope. This records agent agreement, not human agreement.

Advisory ownership excludes cooperating checkpoint writers; hostile filesystem
writers are outside the guarantee. Receipts bind bytes, not their truth.
Recovery preserves lock/checkpoint/receipt/artefacts, while actual branch and
worktree preservation remains the caller's duty. Heartbeats cover short
checkpoint operations and do not supervise long-running clients. Directory
flush failure can occur after release; inspect preserved state before retrying.
