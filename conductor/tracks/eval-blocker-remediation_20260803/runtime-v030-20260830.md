## Boundaries and next work

Fixed `--version` and `--help` diagnostics are not model inference, study
observations, condition admission, protocol freeze or scoring. Every receipt
keeps `admitted` and `study_unlocked` false. Loader reports are trusted local
diagnostics, not tamper-proof attestation; OS/driver bytes and network egress
are not independently attested. Temporary output storage is not a disk quota.

Next: verify a fixed synthetic output-capture mode, implement deterministic
normalisation and the actual study runner, bind its complete component closure
to a committed protocol, then implement affirmative admission. Unfinished
engineering does not justify the no-admissible-condition fallback. Clinical,
legal, policy, regulatory, employment, cultural-safety, organisational and
deployment validation remain external.

Rollback the explicit module and shared extension together after checking
downstream consumers; retain all historical and new diagnostic receipts.
