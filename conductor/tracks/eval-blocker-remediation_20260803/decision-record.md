Date: 2026-08-03
Status: recommended plan pending external execution

## Option A — Full primary evaluation (Recommended)

Complete and admit H0–H8 evidence under the canonical manifest, then run Track
5 scoring/IRR and Track 6 analysis.

Rationale: preserves the approved study design, supports complete condition
comparisons, and keeps provenance and human-evaluator requirements intact.

Contingency: if a harness remains unavailable, keep that condition quarantined;
do not substitute H8P or infer missing runs. Escalate the unresolved owner and
reassess whether the protocol can still support full coverage.

## Option B — Formally amended reduced-scope evaluation

Exclude irrecoverable conditions through an explicit protocol amendment, revise
the manifest and acceptance criteria, and analyse only the admitted scope.

Rationale: produces a bounded result when external capacity is permanently
unavailable.

Contingency: report exclusions, missingness, reduced denominators, and the
amendment prominently; do not compare excluded conditions or claim full study
coverage.

## Option C — Evidence-readiness closeout

Close implementation with the protocol, fixtures, validation receipts,
quarantine inventory, and governance handoff, without Track 5/6 results.

Rationale: preserves useful engineering and governance outputs when evidence
cannot ethically or operationally be obtained.

Contingency: retain the handoff as the next-start package and do not publish
performance, safety, clinical, human-evaluator, or comparative conclusions.

## Decision rule

Remain on Option A while external execution is reasonably recoverable. Move to
Option B only after an authorised protocol decision documents irrecoverability.
Use Option C when neither full nor amended evidence execution is authorised or
feasible. The automation cannot make the owner, human-evaluator, licence, or
protocol decision.

## Immediate actions

1. Send the external request to each H2–H8 owner.
2. Collect raw evidence and attestations without altering originals.
3. Re-run the manifest audit after each submission batch.
4. Present the decision record to the authorised study owner if any condition
   remains unrecoverable.
