# H2 — Portable HEAPS guidance and original report mapping

Status: implemented and content-reviewed on the HEAPS branch; coordinator
integration and merge remain separate. This receipt covers guidance, mapping,
portable navigation and original-template admission. It does not qualify an H4
assembler, an official HEAPS form or an operational clinical workflow.

## Delivered behaviour

The portable [HEAPS guide](../../../skills/rca-investigation/references/methods/heaps.md)
works across triage, investigate, report and track. It records local process and
human method selection before analytical tools; sources and accounts before
analysis; alternative explanations, contrary evidence and source-family lineage;
and distinct participation, review and authority. Staff involvement is recorded
separately from facilitation, challenge and approval responsibilities.

The [original preparation outline](../../../skills/rca-investigation/assets/templates/markdown/15-heaps-review-draft.md)
provides a plain-language opening, evidence/source-family register, clinical
chronology with uncertainty, factor and finding sections, recommendation/action
trace, evaluation measures, unresolved questions and a suggested meeting agenda.
It is an original synthetic working aid. Its headings and agenda do not claim to
reconstruct current Queensland Health or CHHHS HEAPS fields or process steps.

The canonical mapping preserves the existing closed safety-work 1.1 model:

- Accounts, findings, recommendations and decisions are statements with distinct
  kinds; no invented core account/finding collection is added.
- Clinical chronology references statements and profile time annotations;
  canonical workflow events are not clinical occurrence records.
- Condition references resolve through the factor's statement ID when starting
  from a factor; factor classification is separate from factor function/status.
- Richer action resources, dependencies, dates and measures remain in the profile,
  with implementation and effectiveness independently evidenced.
- The later manifest, packet decision and status receipt remain detached from the
  case/profile/report they identify. Record/reference checks and D0 byte-span
  checks retain different scopes.
