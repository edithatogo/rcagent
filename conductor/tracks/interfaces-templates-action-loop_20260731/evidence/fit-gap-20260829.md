# Track 09 fit-gap receipt

Date: 2026-08-29

## Selection

The authoritative incident platform remains external. The repository adds only a client-neutral JSON registry, deterministic validators, generated-synthetic views, original Apache-2.0 template manifests, method-selection guidance, and dry-run adapter receipts. It does not create a case database, workflow engine, enterprise connector, messaging surface, approval service, or organisation-specific user interface.

ims+ and comparable platforms are treated as systems of record. Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams, Power Automate, Dataverse, Flowable/CMMN/BPMN/DMN, and FHIR exchanges remain optional contract-only or unavailable adapters until an applicable authority approves configuration, data, endpoints, identity, retention and deployment. Safe fallback is local preparation with no endpoint, credential, message, mutation, lodgement, closure or approval.

## Dependency lifecycle

- Locked core: `tools/interface_actions.py`, `evaluation/interfaces/registry.json`, schema 1.0.
- Optional adapters: replaceable dry-run read/import/export/reconcile contracts; failure returns a negative receipt.
- System of record: external approved platform; no shadow registry.
- Compatibility: schema 1.0 and canonical safety-work 1.x; drift is caught by schema and contract tests.
- Removal condition: remove local gaps when an approved maintained platform provides equivalent provenance, privacy, recovery and effectiveness-separation semantics.

## Rights and legacy material

The Track 09 registry admits only newly authored Apache-2.0 template manifests. Existing investigation templates containing blanket confidentiality or operational timing language are quarantined from this surface. This is not a legal rights clearance or policy validation.

## Gates retained

No clinical, policy, legal, regulatory, employment, cultural-safety, organisational, deployment, identifiable-data or external-action approval is inferred. Agent-panel agreement is research review evidence only.
