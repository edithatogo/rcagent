# Decision: Agent panels for remaining repository reviews

- **Decision ID:** 20260830-001-legacy-agent-review
- **Status:** Approved with conditions
- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Owner:** Repository owner
- **Scope:** Remaining repository engineering, admission-evidence, research,
  scoring-method, reproducibility, client-conformance and closure reviews

## Authorisation and precedence

The owner directed: “Substitute any external reviews by humans by sending to a
panel of subagents and changing the plan.” This extends the approved
[standing research governance](./20260829-004-agent-panel-research-governance.md)
to remaining repository reviews. It supersedes requirements to recruit
independent human reviewers for those duties. Historical receipts remain intact.

The owner reaffirmed this rule on 2026-08-30 and approved the prospective study
with readiness fallback. Do not request reviewer recruitment, repeat review
authorisation or routine phase approvals. Reuse existing exact-scope decisions;
escalate only a genuinely new reserved action or material scope change.

## Review protocol

- Use at least three agents with explicit acceptance, evidence-integrity and
  safety/privacy roles. Record exact input revision, instructions, findings,
  disagreement, abstention and disposition. Disclose unavailable model revision
  rather than inventing one.
- For numerical research scoring, use three blind scoring agents and a separate
  post-submission adjudicator. Freeze submissions before sharing peer ratings.
  Record input/rubric hashes, individual ratings and correlated-error limits.
- Call the result agent agreement, never human agreement or independence.
- Any unresolved hard-gate finding blocks a positive recommendation; votes or
  aggregate agreement cannot override missing evidence or safety violations.
- Agent review of client compatibility requires actual client execution receipts.
  Agent review of historical provenance cannot attest to an unobserved run.
- Version rubric changes prospectively, retain all original scores and negative
  results, and use a new blinded evaluation. Never revise scores until they pass.
- The standing numerical thresholds apply only to synthetic internal regression
  evidence, not automatically to historical real-case studies.

## Boundaries

H8 Human Expert is a study condition, not a reviewer appointment. It remains
unverified/unadmitted for study use unless genuine provenance-complete human
outputs pass admission; no execution or completion is inferred. H8P stays separate.
Excluding or redefining study conditions requires the
[prospective protocol decision](./20260830-002-prospective-agent-study.md).

No human external-review recruitment is required for repository completion.
Clinical, legal, policy, regulatory, employment, cultural-safety, organisational
and deployment validation stay outside that completion. Applicable authority,
credentials, spending, rights, private-data admission and publication are not
delegated to an agent vote. Existing release/submission approvals remain valid
within their exact scope; this decision neither revokes nor broadens them.
