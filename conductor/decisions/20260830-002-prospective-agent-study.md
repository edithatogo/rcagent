## Recommendation

Approve Option B: a new, versioned, research-only evaluation using admitted
synthetic cases and admitted executable conditions, with blind agent-panel
scoring and post-submission adjudication. Preserve historical H0–H8 and H8P
unchanged. Exclude unavailable human-comparator comparisons rather than naming
agent outputs H8. Use readiness-only Option C if no condition is admissible.

The review substitution is already approved; this decision is about the study
question, condition identity, denominator and claims, not reviewer recruitment.

## Authorised implementation

1. Assign a new study ID; version condition/case manifests and denominators.
2. Freeze eligible inputs, scoring rubric, blinding method and conservative
   non-operational thresholds before any scoring. Do not reuse old results as a
   new condition or apply synthetic thresholds to real-case clinical claims.
3. Replace prose-based preflights with affirmative hash-bound admission and
   completion receipts. Separate admission-before-blinding from scoring-start.
4. Add synthetic positive and adversarial fixtures that can never count as
   observed primary study slots; produce a current eligibility inventory.
5. Preserve H8/H8P identity, missingness, exclusion reasons and all historical
   raw evidence. Do not claim human-comparator agreement or a clinical standard.

## Evidence, impact and safe default

The three-agent panel identified review substitutions as safe, but missing
execution evidence remains. The historical audit reports zero eligible slots;
this is not a fresh inventory claim. Current preflight scripts inspect negative
prose and/or a nonempty CSV rather than affirmative hash-bound admission.

The change is reversible by retaining separate study artefacts; it introduces
additional manifest/fixture maintenance but no new platform. Default data is
generated synthetic. No new credentials, provider execution, paid compute,
rights exception, private data or publication is authorised here. Any use of
existing authority must be checked against the exact artefact and action.

The pre-approval safe default was to preserve the historical protocol and keep
scoring/unblinding blocked. Approval is now recorded: continue preflight
hardening, synthetic fixtures, read-only eligibility inventory and root
acceptance mapping. Study execution waits for implementation and admission,
not another protocol decision.
