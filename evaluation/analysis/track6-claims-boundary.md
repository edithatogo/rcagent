# Track 6 Claims Boundary

## Permitted now

- Report that the protocol and pilot calibration exist.
- Report the number and location of observed raw/normalized artefacts.
- Report unresolved provenance, blinding, metadata, and operator/human gates.
- Report that comparative statistics and publication conclusions are pending.

## Not permitted now

- Comparative condition performance claims.
- Skill-effect or model-effect claims.
- Effect sizes, correlations, kappa results, or failure-mode frequencies as final findings.
- Publication, clinical-governance, or safety conclusions based on provisional rows.

Substantive Track 6 work starts only after Track 5 provides a valid, provenance-complete, unblinded final dataset.
