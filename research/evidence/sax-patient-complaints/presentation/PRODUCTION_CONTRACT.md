This is a production and acceptance specification, not a legal agreement. It turns the agreed evidence-to-practice outline into individually assignable slide contracts. The assumed audience is clinicians, medical leaders and clinical-governance staff. Audience, event, authorised branding and current presenter disclosures remain to be confirmed; do not invent them.

## Delivery boundary

Ten main slides occupy 18 minutes; a further two minutes are reserved for questions, with S10 remaining visible. Four hidden backup slides are available on demand and are not part of the timed talk. Present the 2022/2024 research, not a claim to have updated the literature to 2026. The original report and journal review are related, differently scoped syntheses, not independent evidence sets.

## Shared mandatory requirements

1. **Evidence:** every factual claim, numerical value and study-derived label requires a source and section/table locator. Distinguish published findings, presenter interpretation, proposed practice and hypothetical examples. Do not manufacture effect estimates, independent sample sizes, formal certainty grades, legal conclusions or local performance data.
2. **Structure:** exactly S01–S10 plus hidden B01–B04. Discussion holds S10. No additional title, agenda or questions slide may silently increase the running order.
3. **Timing:** interactions are included in each slide allocation. Rehearse the complete script; word budgets alone are not timing evidence. Protect the conclusion and question period.
4. **Format:** 16:9, 13.333 × 7.5 inches, safe margins at least 0.5 inches. Titles 32–40 pt, main text 24–30 pt, chart/table labels normally at least 22 pt, references 14–16 pt. Backup tables may use 20 pt. Solve overflow by editing or moving detail, not shrinking text.
5. **Editing:** native PowerPoint layouts/placeholders for ordinary titles and text; editable native charts, tables and shapes for bespoke displays. Remove unused, duplicated or off-slide objects. Never flatten a whole slide into an image.
6. **Accessibility:** meaningful alt text, logical reading order, direct labels and no colour-only coding. Internal contrast targets: 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text; these targets do not constitute a claim of certified accessibility.
7. **Static export:** every slide must make sense without animation. No macros, essential network access or unsupported media. Optional reveals must preserve the hypothetical-data label and a comprehensible final PDF state.
8. **Identity:** use an authorised event/institutional master if supplied; otherwise a neutral master. Do not recreate logos, imply institutional endorsement or present publication-era affiliations as current affiliations.
9. **Citations:** first-use order is [1] report, [2] journal review, [3] supplement. Reuse those numbers across main and backup slides. Full citations, locators and qualifications belong in speaker notes. Do not import the paper's internal citation numbering without adding the corresponding sources.
10. **Notes:** each slide requires purpose, narration, qualifications, sources, transition, interaction instructions and a short-delivery fallback. Narration ranges below exclude optional backup use.
11. **Fairness and safety:** use fictional/synthetic teaching material only. No real complaint identifiers, diagnostic-risk profiles or conclusions about complaint credibility. Current clinical safety takes priority over administrative complaint handling.
12. **Rights:** preserve source-specific rights and attribute adaptations. The repository licence does not relicense third-party publications. Do not claim all source binaries have been acquired.
13. **Production deliverables:** editable PPTX, PDF of the ten main slides, accessible speaker notes, source/claim register, chart data and QA receipt. These are future slide-production outputs, not outputs already completed by this specification.
14. **Release:** inspect every rendered slide; verify notes, links, references and static exports; record timed rehearsal. Open the PPTX in PowerPoint on Mac and Android before claiming those platforms tested, recording application versions. Untested checks remain NOT RUN.
15. **Change control:** any change to evidence, source version, ordering, timing, numerical content or slide count requires a contract revision and rechecking affected dependencies. Presenter approval of interpretations and recommendations is separate from technical validation or repository CI.

## Source-reconciliation rules

- SRC01 reports 44 included studies; SRC02 reports 25. Use the journal set for the main narrative. Do not pool the counts or assert that exactly 19 studies were simply removed: eligibility and search updates differ.
- SRC02's abstract reports 8,079 records/250 full texts, while the main Results reports nearly 8,900/255. Omit those figures and do not reconstruct PRISMA until reconciled against original records or a documented correction.
- Search-update wording differs between July and September 2023. Say updated in 2023 unless author records resolve it.
- The abstract says six intervention types, but the main Results and Tables 4–5 name seven. Use the seven named categories, with this discrepancy retained in production notes.
- Table 4/Results include 18 intervention publications; Table 5's caption says 17. Use 18 for the study-design denominator. Category assignments total 19 because Cardoso (2017) appears in two categories; do not mistake category sums for unique studies.
- Use the specific Methods statement: screening/appraisal in pairs, extraction by one reviewer checked by another. Do not repeat the Discussion's broader assertion that every stage was independently duplicated.
- Different diagnostic groupings were used in the two surgical studies involving mental-health-related conditions. Do not convert this into a uniform diagnosis-specific causal effect, a patient prediction model or a rule about complaint merit.
- The article's evidence-strength wording is not a new GRADE assessment. Do not combine design-specific appraisal yes-counts into a common quality percentage.
- Included publications contain systematic reviews as well as primary studies. Do not treat the 25 as independent cohorts, sum overlapping patient samples or call all included studies trials.

**Visual:** sparse opening slide; optional care/response/access labels. No invented trend, numerical claim, cover-page collage or decorative photograph. Alt text describes the opening question.

**Notes:** 80–100 words; allow up to 10 seconds for a show of hands or reflection. Offer alternative explanations for a lower count as hypotheses, not local findings. Transition: “We asked two different questions, and they lead to different kinds of decisions.” Fallback: omit the show of hands.

**Accept only if:** question is visually dominant; no improvement is asserted; interaction and narration fit 60 seconds. No external citation should make this framing appear to be an observed result.

|---|---:|---:|---:|

## S08 — What would success look like for this patient?

**Time:** 12:30–15:00. **Purpose:** apply the evidence to a fictional concern. **Classes:** fictional teaching example and proposed response.

**Required copy:** “Fictional teaching scenario”; “After surgery, a patient reports ongoing concerns, inconsistent explanations and repeated contacts. The team believes it has explained the issue. The patient feels unheard.” Question: “What would count as a successful response?” Path: “Check safety → clarify → investigate and respond → verify learning.” Body ceiling: 65 words.

**Visual:** scenario card plus four-step proposed response. No patient photograph, actual patient quote, diagnosis label or institutional logo. Alt text calls both scenario and response what they are: fictional and proposed.

**Notes:** 170–205 words plus up to 30 seconds for reflection/one answer, inside the 150-second budget. Check whether current concerns require clinical assessment before administrative handling. Coordinate contact, identify substantive issues and preserve distinct allegations/contact records. Do not assume either party is correct or infer negligence. Contrast fast closure with an understood response and checked learning. Transition to service measures. Fallback: rhetorical question without audience discussion.

**Evidence:** presenter-authored scenario and synthesis; no claim that this bundle was tested in the review.

**Accept only if:** fictional label is visible; no identifiers appear; urgent safety is not deferred; no case finding or validated-protocol claim is made; timing is respected.

## S09 — What I would change—and measure

**Time:** 15:00–17:00. **Purpose:** propose balanced, operationally defined evaluation. **Class:** presenter proposal, not published validation.

**Required copy:** “Proposed local evaluation—not a validated framework”; “Access: ability to raise a concern”; “Response: meaningful response time”; “Learning: recurrence of a defined safety problem”; “Organisational effects: staff time and experience.” Body ceiling: 50 words.

**Visual:** four equally weighted domains with one candidate measure each. No invented dashboard values, targets, RAG ratings or institutional commitments. Alt text explicitly calls the framework proposed.

**Required metric definitions in notes:**

- Access: respondents who know how to raise a concern divided by eligible respondents answering that item. Report survey frame, response rate, missingness and approved disaggregation. Complaints records alone cannot estimate this.
- Response: working days per episode to first meaningful response, excluding automated acknowledgements. Report pending episodes, eligible totals and missing timestamps rather than silently excluding them.
- Learning/care: confirmed recurrence of a pre-specified safety problem per relevant care opportunity/encounter, scaled to 1,000 where appropriate. Define ascertainment, window and clinical review; show rare-event uncertainty and suitable comparison. Complaint counts are not confirmed-harm counts.
- Organisational effects: measured staff hours per defined complaint episode, with a staff-experience balancing measure. Report usable/eligible episodes and missingness; predefine roles, boundaries and sampling. No assumed savings or invented unit costs.

**Notes:** 180–210 words. Propose a scoped pilot with baseline, accountable owner, consumer input and pre-specified evaluation—not an approved programme. Distinguish contacts, episodes, issues, clinical events and claims; link records without erasing originals or changing counting rules mid-series. Use comparison data where feasible. Transition to the opening question. Fallback: four domains and one worked definition.

**Accept only if:** measures are operationally defined; no local result/target/approval is invented; access and learning remain alongside speed/burden; denominators and missingness are explicit; no complaint-suppression target or diagnostic-risk profiling appears.
